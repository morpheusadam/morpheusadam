## 👋 About Me

I'm **Morpheus Adam** — a web developer and a cheerful hacker from **Iran**. I build full-stack web applications with a focus on **PHP**, **Laravel**, and **Go**, and I enjoy crafting **e-commerce platforms, content-management systems, and developer tools**.

- 🛠️ I work mostly with **Laravel**, **PHP**, and **Go**, backed by **MySQL/MariaDB**, **Redis**, and **Linux**.
- 🌐 I love **API development & integration**, **modular architecture**, and shipping **RTL / Persian-first** experiences.
- 🚀 I enjoy building cool stuff, exploring new tech, and contributing to open source.
- 📫 Reach me at **morpheusadam95@gmail.com** or visit **[sam.zeonic.me](https://sam.zeonic.me)**.
